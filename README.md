## Overview

The goal of the BEACON project is to respond to an enormous cybersecurity workforce shortage as well as gaps in available university-level cybersecurity programs by offering a vertically-aligned, hands-on, multitiered, adversarial thinking-based approach to the cybersecurity undergraduate curriculum. We help to satisfy needs of the cybersecurity community (university students and instructors, industry) by developing three lab modules that incorporate industry best practices and tools, and address both relevant knowledge as well as hands-on practical skills to simulate real-world cyber problems and environments.
